Styliste: Makeover Merge - The Ultimate Fashion & Beauty Game

About This Game

Welcome to Styliste: Makeover Merge, where your creativity and sense of style shape the ultimate fashion experience! In this game, you’ll take on the role of a professional stylist, transforming everyday looks into stunning fashion statements.

But it's not just about picking outfits—your skills go beyond fashion! Merge beauty tools, unlock exclusive styling accessories, and perfect your craft to give your clients confidence-boosting makeovers. With a rich storyline, diverse characters, and exciting challenges, Styliste: Makeover Merge offers a unique blend of fashion, strategy, and storytelling.

Get ready to step into the world of beauty and style, where every decision you make changes the game!
